# Coding-in-a-Connected-World-Development-of-Computational-Thinking-Skills-Q1-Project-Proposal

In today's fast-paced academic environment, students are constantly managing complex workloads spread across various subjects, projects, and assignments. Without a centralized organization system, tracking deadlines and prioritizing tasks through physical notebooks, mental reminders, or disjointed group chats often leads to crucial assignments slipping through the cracks. This results in last-minute cramming, missed deadlines, elevated stress, and compromised academic performance.   

To solve this issue, the Task and Assignment Scheduling Kit (TASK) is designed as an automated task management platform tailored specifically for student productivity. By leveraging computational thinking principles, TASK simplifies workflow management through automated priority calculations, direct integration with Google Classroom and Google Calendar, and visual productivity analytics. Through features like high-priority deadline warnings and an intelligent urgency-sorting matrix, TASK empowers students to move away from reactive studying and maintain consistent control over their academic progress.
