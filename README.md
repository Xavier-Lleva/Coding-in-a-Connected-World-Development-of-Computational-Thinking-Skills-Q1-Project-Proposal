# Coding-in-a-Connected-World-Development-of-Computational-Thinking-Skills-Q1-Project-Proposal

In today's academic environment, students are constantly managing complex workloads spread across various subjects, projects, and assignments. Without a centralized organization system, tracking deadlines and prioritizing tasks through physical notebooks, mental reminders, or group chats often leads to important assignments slipping through the cracks. This can lead to last-minute cramming, missed deadlines, elevated stress, and poor academic performance.   

To solve this issue, the Task and Assignment Scheduling Kit (TASK) is designed as an automated task management platform specifically for student productivity. By utilizing computational thinking, TASK simplifies workflow management through automated priority calculations, direct integration with Google Classroom and Google Calendar, and visual productivity analytics. Through features like high-priority deadline warnings and a sorting matrix, TASK empowers students to move away from reactive studying and maintain consistent control over their academic progress.
